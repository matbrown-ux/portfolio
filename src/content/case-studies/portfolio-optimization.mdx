<Chapter title="Overview" asideLabel="Services" aside={frontmatter.services}>

Portfolio Optimization is a service inside Vamp Network for UGC creators. A creator gets a custom portfolio site on their own domain, and I design, write and build every one of them myself.

Vamp Network launched it at the end of July 2026, once its management and performance services had gained enough traction to support a new one.

</Chapter>

<Chapter title="The challenge">

A brand looks at a creator's portfolio before it ever replies to a pitch. Many UGC creators send sixty clips in a shared folder, or a link that looks like every other creator's. That tells the brand the creator is still figuring things out, however good the work is.

Vamp Network manages creators every day, so the team already knew how creators pitch and what brands look for. The service had to turn that into something a creator could buy: a professional site that works for them every time they pitch.

<PullQuote text="A brand looks at the portfolio before it ever replies to the pitch." />

</Chapter>

<Chapter title="The approach">

Each build starts from what the creator already has and what they like. They bring their portfolio assets. Reference sites, color palettes and logos they are drawn to, and a brand voice document, are optional but shape the result.
